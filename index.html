About Me

I am a Telecommunications Engineer and PhD in Information and Communication Technologies, with a research background in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning.

My work focuses on the dynamic resource management of wireless IoT networks, particularly LoRaWAN, applying Machine Learning and Reinforcement Learning to optimization and autonomous decision-making problems. I have also worked on advanced logistics, developing AI-based solutions for optimization and decision-making in dynamic environments.

My research combines AI, simulation and optimization, with a particular interest in Reinforcement Learning, Multi-Agent Systems and autonomous decision-making. I have participated in national and European R&D projects and have published my work in international journals and conferences.
